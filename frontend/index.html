Credential Desk

Registration, issuance, presentation, verification and revocation against a permissioned Hyperledger Fabric ledger.

Session not signed in

Register an identity

The issuer verifies the applicant's information out of band, then commits only a SHA-256 digest of it to the ledger. The fields below stay in the off-chain store.

Issue a credential

Creates a signed credential, records its hash and ACTIVE status on-chain, and returns a short-lived QR presentation token.

Holder wallet

Credentials held by this identity, read back from the ledger. Regenerating a presentation mints a fresh token that expires in five minutes.

Verify a presentation

Two independent checks. The local check proves the issuer's signature and works without connectivity. Only the ledger check can see a revocation.

    Reconcile offline acceptances

    A verifier that accepted credentials while offline replays their IDs here once connectivity returns. Anything no longer ACTIVE is flagged as a stale acceptance.

    Revoke a credential

    Restricted to the issuing organisation. The credential is not deleted — its status changes to REVOKED and every later verification rejects it.

    Credential history

    Every committed transaction that touched this key, taken from the ledger itself.

    Measured performance

    Latencies and transaction counts collected by the backend for the evaluation table.
